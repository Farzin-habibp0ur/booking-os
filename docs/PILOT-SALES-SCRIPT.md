# Pilot Sales Script — AI Front Desk

> **Audience:** Founder running discovery + demo + pilot pitch (Year 1, you). Future founding AE.
> **Purpose:** The complete sales script — discovery questions, the 60-second pitch, and 5 objection responses — for converting a Year-1 ICP medical-spa owner into a pilot applicant.
> **Pair with:** [`DEMO_SCRIPT.md`](../DEMO_SCRIPT.md) (the 12-minute wedge demo) and [`docs/PILOT-OPS.md`](PILOT-OPS.md) (what happens after they say yes).
> **Last updated:** 2026-05-09

---

## Use this script for

- **Discovery + demo combined call.** Single 30-minute call. 5 minutes discovery, 12 minutes demo, 13 minutes pitch + Q&A + close. Async loom is acceptable for prospects who can't take a live call.

If the prospect is not Year-1 ICP (med spa, US/CA, owner-operated, 1–10 locations, $800K–$2.5M, public Instagram), redirect to the Year 2 waitlist before running this script. ICP discipline matters more than any single closed deal.

## Discovery (5 minutes)

Open with: "Before I show you anything, I'd love to understand your clinic for a few minutes. Quick questions, then I'll demo."

### The six discovery questions

1. **"What kinds of treatments do you offer most — injectables, laser, body contouring?"**
   - _Why:_ qualifies `practiceType=MED_SPA`. If they answer derm, plastic surgery, hair restoration, IV/wellness, dental — they're Year 2.
2. **"How many locations are you running?"**
   - _Why:_ in-ICP is 1–10. Larger chains and PE-backed operators are out of scope for the pilot stage.
3. **"Roughly what's a typical month for you in bookings? In revenue?"**
   - _Why:_ grounds the baseline math. Expected: 60–200 bookings/month, $65K–$210K/month for $800K–$2.5M annual.
4. **"Where are most of your inbound leads coming from? Instagram DMs, WhatsApp, the website, walk-ins?"**
   - _Why:_ confirms the wedge applies. If they say "exclusively walk-ins, we don't run social," they may not be a fit.
5. **"Who replies to those messages today, and at what hours?"**
   - _Why:_ surfaces after-hours / unanswered-threshold pain. The owner's answer here is the wedge moment.
6. **"Are you currently using anything for after-hours coverage or auto-replies?"**
   - _Why:_ sets up objection #1 ("we already use [other tool]") if they have one.

### What you're listening for

- A real pain around inbound message coverage, not just "we want more bookings."
- A specific recent example they bring up unprompted ("just last Saturday I missed a DM from someone who wanted lip filler").
- Frustration with their PMS doing one job (booking) but not the other (messaging).
- Confidence that their staff would adopt approve-the-draft if it actually saved time.

If they don't have message-coverage pain, the wedge won't land. Don't run the demo. Politely close: "Sounds like your front desk is dialled in. We're built for clinics with the kind of inbound message volume that's outpacing staff coverage. If that ever changes, here's our pilot link." Move on.

## The 60-second pitch (after the demo)

Keep this verbatim or close to it:

> "Here's what we just walked through. Business Command Centre is the **AI Front Desk for medical spas**. One inbox for Instagram, WhatsApp, SMS, email, and your website chat. Every inbound message gets an AI-drafted reply within seconds — clinic-safe, calendar-aware — and your front desk approves with one tap. The AI also fills cancellations from your waitlist, follows up on consults that didn't book a treatment, and chases expired quotes.
>
> The only ROI screen we show is the dashboard you just saw: **bookings captured** and **bookings that would have been missed without us**. Two cards. Visible to you and to us. We capture a baseline before we start — your number for monthly bookings — so the comparison is honest.
>
> The pilot is **30 days, free, concierge — I personally onboard you on a 60-minute call**. We have 5 slots. If at the end of 30 days we've handled at least 10 messages, you got at least 5 platform bookings, and you want to continue, you graduate at $397 a month for one location, $197 a month for each additional location. If we didn't, you walk away. No contract. No card on file. Kill switch any time."

End with one question, slow and direct: "Does the wedge sound real to you?"

If yes → close. If they hesitate → ride the silence. Don't fill it.

## The close

> "Want me to send you the pilot application now? You can fill it out tonight, I'll respond within 24 hours, and we can do the concierge call early next week."

If yes → send https://businesscommandcentre.com/pilot in chat / follow-up email immediately. Confirm receipt.

If "let me think about it" → "Totally fair. What's the one thing that has to be true for this to be a yes?" Listen. That's the real objection. Address it (objection bank below) or schedule a follow-up.

If "we're not ready" → ask why. If it's timing (e.g., end of fiscal quarter, owner traveling), schedule a 30-day follow-up. If it's structural (e.g., they want HIPAA), kill cleanly and route to Year 2 waitlist.

## Five objection responses

### 1. "We already use [other tool]" — channel coverage + attribution proof

**Lines:**

> "Most clinics our size already have something. The question is what it covers. Most booking systems are excellent at booking — Boulevard, Vagaro, Mangomint, all good. They're not built for inbound message coverage across Instagram, WhatsApp, SMS, email, web chat. And none of them prove the bookings they would have missed without their involvement, on a dashboard.
>
> We sit in front of those messages. Your booking system stays where it is. We track every booking we capture, and the subset that would have been missed without us, against the baseline you set with me on the call. The dashboard is the proof.
>
> If after 30 days the dashboard isn't moving, you walk. If it is, you've got attribution data your current stack doesn't give you."

**Don't:** trash the competitor by name. We're not displacing their PMS — we're sitting in front of it. That framing reduces switching anxiety.

---

### 2. "How is this HIPAA compliant?" — non-PHI infrastructure, PMS stays system of record

**Lines:**

> "Honest answer: in Year 1, we're not HIPAA compliant, and we don't sign Business Associate Agreements. We deliberately built BCC as **non-clinical infrastructure**. Your PMS stays the system of record for any patient health information. We don't store medical records, before/after photos, treatment histories, or aftercare protocols. We removed those from the schema entirely.
>
> What we handle is the inbound message + the booking shell — names, contact info, services booked, prices paid. The same kind of data your front desk handles in a phone call.
>
> If you require a BAA today, I'll be straight with you: we're the wrong fit. We're working toward a HIPAA path in Year 2 once we have BAAs lined up with our subprocessors. Want me to add you to the Year 2 waitlist?"

**Tone:** confident, not apologetic. The non-PHI stance is a deliberate product decision. It's a feature, not a missing feature.

If they push: link to [`docs/COMPLIANCE-POSTURE.md`](COMPLIANCE-POSTURE.md).

---

### 3. "Why $397?" — cost vs missed bookings + staff time saved

**Lines:**

> "$397 is one med-spa booking. The dashboard shows you a count of bookings we captured that would have been missed — the average across early pilots is around X per month at your size. _(Substitute real numbers once you have them. Until then, frame as: a single recovered Botox booking covers it; a recovered laser session covers it twice.)_
>
> The other half of the math is staff time. Most front desks we've watched spend 30–60 minutes a day reading and replying to Instagram DMs, WhatsApp, and email. The AI drafts those replies in seconds. Your team approves with one tap. That's roughly an hour a day back to a $25–$35/hour staff member — call it $500–$700/month in time alone, before we count any captured bookings.
>
> If after 30 days we haven't paid for ourselves on the dashboard, you walk. The $397 is the post-pilot graduation price. The pilot itself is free."

**Avoid:** ROI calculators that require speculative math. Anchor on the dashboard and on staff time saved.

---

### 4. "I want my staff to write replies, not AI" — staff approval mode is default

**Lines:**

> "That's the default mode and it stays the default mode through the entire pilot. The AI drafts; your staff approves. Auto-send is **off**, per channel, until you explicitly turn it on. Even after pilot, auto-reply is opt-in per channel — you can have it on for web chat (where it's lowest risk) and off for Instagram (where the tone matters most), or any combination.
>
> What the AI saves is the **typing**, not the **judgment**. Your front desk reads the draft, taps approve or edits, sends. The fast ones do it in 5 seconds.
>
> The clinics that get value the fastest are exactly the ones whose staff want to stay in the loop on every message. The AI lifts the volume; the human owns the voice. Want me to show you how the approval UI feels?"

If yes → walk them through the inbox approval UI on the demo tenant. If they're still skeptical, offer to send [`docs/AI-FRONT-DESK-OPERATOR-GUIDE.md`](AI-FRONT-DESK-OPERATOR-GUIDE.md) so their team can read it before the call.

---

### 5. "What if it doesn't work?" — 30-day free pilot, kill switch, no contract

**Lines:**

> "That's why the pilot is 30 days, free, concierge, no card on file, no contract.
>
> Three things have to be true at the end of 30 days for me to ask you to graduate: we handled at least 10 messages in your inbox, we drove at least 5 platform bookings — or your baseline ÷ 12 if your baseline is small — and you tell me you want to continue. If any of those aren't true, the pilot ends. There's no auto-charge. There's no contract to wriggle out of.
>
> The honest cost to you is the 60-minute concierge call to get set up, plus 15 minutes a week with me on a check-in. If we don't hit the scorecard, you walked away with a free month of attribution data and the experience of seeing what an AI-drafted inbox feels like. That's the worst case."

**Tone:** matter-of-fact. The pilot terms are airtight. Don't oversell — let the deal speak for itself.

## Off-script trapdoors

These are real prospects, off-script. Handle deliberately:

### "Can we customize the AI's tone for our brand?"

> "Yes. The first onboarding step is voice configuration — tone, must-include phrases, must-avoid words. We tune it during the concierge call and adjust during weekly check-ins."

### "Can we use this for outbound campaigns?"

> "Campaigns are gated during pilot. We can talk about turning them on after pilot, once your inbox flow is dialled in. The wedge we're proving is inbound message coverage, not outbound."

### "Do you integrate with our PMS [Boulevard / Mangomint / Vagaro / Mindbody / Zenoti]?"

> "We don't replace your PMS. We sit in front of it. Bookings created through BCC can be pushed to your PMS via [insert specifics — TBD founder]; reads are not currently bidirectional. Your PMS remains your source of truth for clinical and financial records."

> **TBD — confirm with founder.** Specifics on PMS push integrations: which providers are wired today vs roadmap. The current state in Year 1 is intentionally limited; we may need to surface this honestly during sales calls.

### "Can my staff onboard themselves without you?"

> "They can — we have an in-product setup wizard. But during pilot, I personally onboard. The 60-minute concierge call is non-negotiable; it's how I capture your baseline and ensure we're set up to prove the wedge. After graduation, you can onboard new staff yourselves using the operator guide we'll send you."

### "Why are you only doing 5 pilots?"

> "Because I'm onboarding each one personally. I'd rather get 5 right than 50 wrong. After the first cohort, we'll either open more slots or iterate before we do."
